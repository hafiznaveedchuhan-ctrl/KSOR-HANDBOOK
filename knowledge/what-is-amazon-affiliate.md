Amazon Associates is Amazon's in-house affiliate marketing program. When you join, you get unique tracking links for products on Amazon. If someone clicks your link and makes a qualifying purchase within 24 hours, you earn a percentage of the sale price as a commission.

It is one of the most widely used affiliate programs in the world because it covers millions of products and Amazon's conversion rate is high — many visitors already trust the brand and have a stored payment method.

## How Commissions Work

Your commission is a percentage of the product's sale price, set per product category. The percentage is fixed by Amazon and updated periodically; the rates below reflect the current published schedule.

| Product Category | Commission Rate |
|---|---|
| Luxury Beauty, Amazon Coins | 10% |
| Digital Music, Physical Music, Handmade, Digital Videos | 5% |
| Physical Books, Kitchen, Automotive | 4.5% |
| Amazon Fire Tablet, Fire TV Devices, Amazon Echo | 4% |
| Toys, Furniture, Home, Outdoors, Tools, Sports | 3% |
| PC, PC Components, DVD & Blu-Ray | 2.5% |
| Televisions, Digital Video Games | 2% |
| Amazon Fresh, Health & Personal Care | 1% |
| Gift Cards, Wireless Service Plans, Alcoholic Beverages, Digital Kindle | 0% |

A few things that affect your earnings:

- **Cookie window**: The 24-hour window starts when the visitor clicks your link. If they add an item to their cart before the window closes, the cookie extends to 90 days for that item.
- **Bounties**: Some Amazon programs (like Prime sign-ups or Audible trials) pay a flat bounty fee rather than a percentage.
- **Returns**: Commissions are reversed if the buyer returns the product.
- **The whole cart counts**: If someone clicks your link for one product and then buys unrelated items in the same session, you earn commission on all of it.

## Commission Calculation Example

A visitor clicks your link to a $120 outdoor chair (Outdoor Furniture, 3%). They also add a $30 book (Physical Books, 4.5%) to the cart in the same session.

- Chair: $120 × 3% = $3.60
- Book: $30 × 4.5% = $1.35
- **Total earned**: $4.95

## Who Can Join

Amazon Associates is open to content creators, bloggers, publishers, and social media users. The basic requirements are:

- You must have an active website, mobile app, or social media presence where you publish original content.
- Your site must not consist primarily of affiliate links or be under construction at the time of application.
- You must be at least 18 years old.
- You must be a resident of an eligible country (the program operates country-specific versions — Associates in one country does not automatically earn on purchases made on another country's Amazon store).

**The 180-day rule**: After you are accepted, you must generate at least three qualifying sales within your first 180 days. If you do not, Amazon will close your account. You can reapply, but the 180-day clock starts again. This makes it important to have an audience or content plan before signing up.

## How Sign-Up Works

1. Go to the Amazon Associates homepage and click **Sign Up**.
2. Log in with your Amazon account or create one.
3. Enter your website or app URLs.
4. Describe how you drive traffic and how you use affiliate links.
5. Choose your payment method (direct deposit, Amazon gift card, or check).
6. Start generating affiliate links immediately using the SiteStripe toolbar that appears when you browse Amazon while logged into your Associates account.

Amazon reviews your application once you've made your first sale, so you can start adding links right away while your account is pending full approval.
